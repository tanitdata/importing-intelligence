# Importing Intelligence — research repository

Data, source matrices, and figure code behind the Tanitdata Research policy
brief **_Importing Intelligence: Token Dependence, Chokepoints, and What
Production-Centric AI Sovereignty Misses_** (TD-PB-2026-02, v1.0, June 2026).

Authors: Tarek Gasmi · Slim Abdelbari · tanitdata.org

## Read the brief

- **Web edition (tanitdata.org):** <https://tanitdata.org/en/policy-briefs/importing-intelligence/>
- **Archival record of version (Zenodo, DOI):** <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20677200>

This repository is the per-cell system of record for the brief's empirical
claims. The brief states market shares, provider country-availability, payment
rails, token prices, and legal regimes that are **time-sensitive**; everything
here is stamped to its observation date (most claims current to **2026-06-12**)
so the brief can be verified against what the sources said when they were read,
not against whatever the live pages say later.

## How the matrices map to the brief

- **Section 4 provider-availability claims** (e.g. "fifteen of nineteen MENA
  jurisdictions appear on all four major U.S. positive country lists"; the
  Yemen/Anthropic and Sudan/Microsoft single-provider divergences) are read
  directly from `matrices/availability_matrix.csv`, sourced in
  `matrices/availability_sources.md`.
- **Section 4 payment-rail claims** (Stripe onboards one MENA country; Adyen
  none; the Maghreb/Levant central-bank restrictions) come from
  `matrices/payment_matrix.csv` and `payment_sources.md`.
- **Figure 3** is generated by `figures/fig3_token_price_decline_v4.py` from
  `data/token_price_milestones.csv`.

## Reading the cell values

**Availability matrix.** `✓` = officially supported (or, for exclusion-list
providers, not excluded by terms — flagged in the sources file); `partial` =
supported with restrictions or pass-through upstream restrictions; `✗` =
officially excluded (by list-absence or named exclusion); `UNKNOWN` =
documentation does not settle the status. Two evidence types are distinguished
in `availability_sources.md`: positive country lists (OpenAI, Anthropic,
Google, Microsoft) versus terms-of-service exclusion lists (Mistral, Cohere,
OpenRouter, AWS). A `✓` from a positive list is a stronger claim than a `✓`
from an exclusion list.

**Payment matrix.** Cells retain their `[n]` footnote markers, which point to
the numbered per-cell notes in `payment_sources.md`. **Mandatory caveat:**
processor unavailability is evidence of payment-chokepoint *exposure*, not
proof that LLM API payment fails from a country — developers use foreign
entities, prepaid/virtual cards, and resellers. No cell records an observed
API-payment outcome.

## Reproducing the figures

The figure scripts are included as provenance for how each chart was drawn.
They were written to run inside the authors' working project tree and read
their data via relative paths into that tree (e.g. the Figure 3 script reads
the milestone JSON from the project's research notes). To re-run them
standalone you will need to repoint those paths at the files in this
repository — Figure 3's data is `data/token_price_milestones.csv`. They are
published for transparency and audit, not as a turnkey build.

Dependencies: Python 3.11+, `matplotlib`, `numpy`.

## Provenance and limitations

Every empirical source carries known limitations, documented alongside it:

- **Provider concentration** (Figures 2, 4) rests on Menlo Ventures survey
  data. Menlo is an investor in LLM companies including Anthropic; figures are
  survey-based, U.S.-skewed, and vendor-adjacent. See `data/menlo/PROVENANCE.md`.
- **Token prices** (Figure 3) draw on Du (2026), a preprint; the half-life and
  software-vs-hardware decomposition are Du's estimates, not replicated.
- The **availability and payment matrices** are point-in-time as of 2026-06-12.
  Provider lists and central-bank rules change without notice; the `archive/`
  manifest pins what was claimed and when. A small number of pages had no
  capturable snapshot at research time — see `archive/snapshots.csv` notes and
  the limitations sections of the two `*_sources.md` files.
